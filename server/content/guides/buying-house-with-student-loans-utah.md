Student debt doesn't disqualify you. How the payment gets counted is what decides the size of the loan you qualify for.

## Do lenders look at my student loan balance or my payment?

Underwriting cares about your monthly debt-to-income ratio. A $90,000 balance with a $310 payment affects your qualification the same way any other $310 payment does. The balance itself isn't the variable.

That surprises people who assume a large balance is fatal. It usually isn't. A large required payment is what compresses your budget.

## What if my loans are in deferment or income-driven repayment?

Loan programs disagree about how to treat loans in deferment, in forbearance, or on income-driven repayment where the reported payment might be very low or even zero.

Some programs will use the actual reported payment. Some require a calculated percentage of the outstanding balance instead, which can be substantially higher than what you actually pay. The rules differ between conventional, FHA, VA and USDA, and they get revised.

> **Bring the documentation up front** — Your servicer's current statement, your repayment plan type, and the payment that's actually reporting to credit. Those three things determine the answer.

## What should I do with my loans before applying?

Pull your credit and check what payment is reporting on each student loan. It isn't always what you're paying, and a stale reported figure can cost you qualifying power.

If you're on an income-driven plan, get your servicer to document the current payment and the plan type in writing. Underwriters need the paperwork, not a screenshot.

## Should I pay off a student loan before buying?

If you carry several small loans, eliminating the one with the highest monthly payment frees ratio immediately. That's often more useful to your qualification than putting the same money toward the largest balance.

Ask your loan officer to model it both ways before you spend the cash, because in some cases the money does more for you as a down payment or as reserves.

## What does this look like in practice?

Get the file reviewed rather than deciding for yourself that student debt rules you out. The treatment varies enough between programs that the same borrower can qualify for meaningfully different amounts depending on which loan they use.
