# Townhome or detached house?

*Deciding*

![New construction townhome living, dining and kitchen](images/blog-hero-model-home.webp)

This comparison is mostly about money and time. Here's how to run it honestly.

## What does the same money buy in each?

In Davis County, the townhome and the detached house at the same price are usually not the same size, the same age or the same location. A new townhome near a highway corridor and an older detached house further from one can land within a few thousand dollars of each other. Compare those two head to head rather than comparing categories.

## Why is the HOA the real difference?

A townhome payment includes dues. A detached house usually doesn't. But the detached house owner is paying for roofing, siding, paint, lawn care and snow removal out of their own pocket, just on an unpredictable schedule rather than a monthly one.

The question isn't whether you want to pay for maintenance. It's whether you would rather pay a fixed amount every month or a variable amount every few years, and whether you want to be the one arranging it.

> **$172 a month buys back your Saturdays** — Whether that's a good trade depends entirely on what you would otherwise do with them.

## When does the townhome win?

Lower entry price for new construction in the same area. No exterior maintenance to schedule. Usually shared amenities you would never build yourself, like a pickleball court. And on a new build, everything is under warranty at the same time rather than staggered across a thirty-year-old house.

## When does the detached house win?

No shared walls. A yard you control. No association telling you what color to paint the front door or how many vehicles you can park. And historically, more land under the improvement, which is the part of a property that appreciates.

## Which two questions settle it?

**How long are you staying?** Under five years, transaction costs dominate and the lower entry price of a townhome tends to win. Over ten, the land component of a detached house starts to matter more.

**Do you want to be responsible for the roof?** Some people find that reassuring. Some find it exhausting. There's no correct answer, but there's a correct answer for you, and it's usually obvious once you ask it directly.
