Hill drives a large share of the housing demand in Davis County. If you're buying because of it, a few things work differently.

## How long is the commute to Hill Air Force Base really?

Distance to the base is a poor proxy for time to the gate. Which gate you use, when your shift starts and which corridor you take matter more than a straight-line measurement.

Western Davis County has become more viable as the West Davis Highway has come online, because the north-south route no longer forces everyone onto the same handful of arterials. Drive your actual route at your actual departure time before you commit. Once, on a weekday.

## Why does a VA loan fit buyers near Hill AFB?

If you're eligible, a VA loan offers a zero-down option with no monthly mortgage insurance, subject to VA and lender requirements. On a $399,900 purchase, the absence of monthly mortgage insurance alone changes the payment meaningfully compared with a low-down conventional loan.

Entitlement, the funding fee and occupancy requirements all apply, and prior use of your entitlement affects what is available now. Get it reviewed rather than assuming.

> **No monthly mortgage insurance** — The VA advantage that changes the monthly number most, and the one most often left out of online estimates.

## How does PCS timing change whether buying makes sense?

If you might move in two or three years, transaction costs are the dominant variable. Buying still makes sense for plenty of military households, particularly where rent is high and the home is likely to rent well, but it needs to be a decision rather than a default.

If you would keep the home and rent it out, check the association's rental rules before you buy. Many communities cap the percentage of homes that can be rented, and a cap you hit later is a problem you can't solve.

## How do homes near a base resell?

Steady demand from incoming personnel is a genuine advantage of buying near Hill. It also means your future buyer pool skews toward people using VA financing, which is worth knowing when you think about condition and price at resale.

## Can I use a VA loan on a townhome?

VA lending in attached-home communities has its own requirements around the association and the project. It's routine, but it's a step, and it's worth confirming early rather than in week four of a contract.
