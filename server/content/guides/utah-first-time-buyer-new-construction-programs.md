A new build is a different transaction from a resale, and most of the differences are in the timeline rather than the house.

## What’s different about a builder’s contract?

Resale purchases in Utah usually run on a familiar state-approved form. New construction usually runs on the builder's own contract, and it's written to protect the builder. Deposit terms, completion-date language, change-order rules and what happens if the home is late are all worth reading closely or having reviewed.

## How does the build timeline affect my rate?

If completion is months out, you can't lock a rate the way you would on a thirty-day resale closing. Extended locks exist and they cost something. Float-down provisions exist and they cost something too.

Ask early what your options are and what each costs, because the answer changes what you can afford and it's easier to plan for in month one than in month five.

> **Ask about the lock before you sign** — A rate that expires two weeks before your home is finished is a problem you can avoid by asking one question early.

## Are builder incentives tied to using the builder’s lender?

Most builder or seller incentives require you to finance through a preferred lender. That's a legitimate arrangement, and it's also a reason to get a competing quote. Compare the total cost of the incentive-with-preferred-lender route against the no-incentive-with-another-lender route. Sometimes the incentive wins by a wide margin. Sometimes it doesn't.

You're always free to use any lender you choose. The incentive is the thing that's conditioned, not your ability to shop.

## Do I need my own inspection on a new build?

New doesn't mean flawless. A municipal inspection confirms code compliance, which isn't the same as confirming quality. An independent inspection before closing, and again before the end of the first-year warranty period, is cheap relative to what it catches.

## What does the builder warranty cover?

Builder warranties are usually tiered: a short period on workmanship and finishes, longer on systems, longest on structure. Get the document, read the exclusions, and note the deadlines. The one-year mark passes faster than you expect.

## What costs aren’t in the builder’s price?

Window coverings, landscaping if it's your responsibility, appliances if they aren't included, and a fence if you want one. On a first purchase these routinely add up to more than buyers expect.
