# Questions to ask the listing agent

*Open houses*

By your listing agent

*Educational information, not financial or legal advice*

You are allowed to ask anything. Here is a checklist you can read on your phone while you walk through. Some answers may be "I'll find out," and that is fine.

## About the sale

- Why are the owners selling?
- How long has it been on the market, and has the price changed?
- Have there been offers? Is there an offer deadline?
- Is the seller open to certain dates or terms?
- Is anyone living in the home now, and when could I take possession?

## About what's included

- Which appliances, fixtures and window coverings stay?
- Is there anything the seller plans to take?
- Are there any recent repairs or upgrades? Were permits pulled?

## About the house itself

- How old are the roof, furnace, air conditioner and water heater?
- Has there been any water damage, pests or foundation work?
- Is there a seller's disclosure I can read? Are there any inspection reports from earlier?
- Is the home on public sewer or a septic system, and on city water or a well?

## About costs

- What are typical monthly utility bills?
- What were last year's property taxes? Could they change after a sale?
- Is there an HOA or special district, and what are the fees? See the guide "Does this home have an HOA?"
- What does homeowner's insurance cost for a home like this?

Try See My Payment for this home to put the taxes and other numbers into a monthly figure.

## About the surroundings

- How is the noise during the day and at night? Traffic, trains, flights or construction?
- How is the parking and street access?
- Are there any planned projects or road work nearby?

Ask about conditions you can observe and verify. Questions about the people who live nearby are not something an agent can or should answer, and the home's value does not depend on them. Stick to the property, the noise and the facts.

## About next steps

- What's the best way to schedule a second visit or inspection?
- What do you need to see before I make an offer, such as a pre-approval letter?
- Who handles the paperwork and closing?

## A few reminders

- Write down the answers so you can compare homes later.
- Ask for anything important in writing.
- An agent who represents the seller has a duty to the seller. Ask how agency works where you live, and whether you want your own agent. Rules differ by state.

If you'd like more detail on any question, ask. That's what open houses are for.
